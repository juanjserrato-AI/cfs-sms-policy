Carolina Food Service — SMS Alerts Sign-Up

Loris, South Carolina · carolinafoodservice.com · Last updated: July 19, 2026

Carolina Food Service operates an internal SMS program that sends operational alerts to members of our team who ask to receive them. This page explains the program and how to sign up.

What you will receive

  • Meeting and appointment reminders
  • Customer-escalation notices
  • Action-needed notifications from our internal assistant system

Message frequency varies based on business activity. Message and data rates may apply.

How to sign up

  1. Contact the Carolina Food Service office and ask to be added to SMS operational alerts. You can reach us through carolinafoodservice.com or by speaking with the company administrator.
  2. Provide the mobile number you want alerts sent to.
  3. Confirm that you agree to the consent statement below.
  4. You will receive a one-time confirmation text: “Carolina Food Service: You are now opted in to operational alerts. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.”
Consent statement

“I agree to receive operational alert text messages from Carolina Food Service at the mobile number I provide. I understand message frequency varies, that message and data rates may apply, that I can reply HELP for help, and that I can reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages.”

Signing up is entirely voluntary. Consent to receive these text messages is never a condition of employment, of any product or service, or of completing any purchase or transaction. Declining — or opting out later — carries no consequence of any kind.

Stopping messages

Reply STOP to any message to stop immediately. Reply HELP for help. We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile number with third parties for their marketing.
