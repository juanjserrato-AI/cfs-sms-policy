Carolina Food Service — SMS Privacy Policy

Loris, South Carolina · carolinafoodservice.com · Last updated: July 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Carolina Food Service ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our SMS text-message program.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in information with any third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information is used solely to operate the messaging program described below.

Information We Collect

For the SMS program we collect only the mobile phone number you provide and records related to your consent and message delivery (for example, opt-in and opt-out requests).

How We Use Your Information

We use your mobile number solely to send the operational alerts you have consented to receive — including meeting and appointment reminders, customer-escalation notices, and action-needed notifications. We do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Voluntary Consent

Consent is voluntary and is never a condition of employment, of any product or service, or of completing any purchase or transaction. Declining, or withdrawing consent later by replying STOP, carries no consequence.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on business activity.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. These charges are billed by and payable to your mobile service provider.

Opting Out

You may opt out of the SMS program at any time by replying STOP to any message. For help, reply HELP or contact us through carolinafoodservice.com.

How We Protect Your Information

We maintain reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information we hold, and we limit access to it to authorized personnel only.
